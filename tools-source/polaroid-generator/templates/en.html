Guide

A simple way to give digital photos an instant-film feel

From clean social graphics to vintage memories

A light frame, a larger caption area and subtle grain can reproduce the visual language people associate with instant-film prints. Keep the frame straight and clean for brand graphics, or add a small rotation, warm tone and paper texture for a scrapbook look.

  • Travel and café photos
  • Instagram and Facebook posts
  • Blog and newsletter graphics
  • Memory cards and scrapbooks

Built to process the image in your browser

This generator uses JavaScript and Canvas 2D on the visitor's device. The selected image does not need to be sent to an image-processing API, which keeps the tool quick and avoids server-side image storage.

Very large phone photos are downscaled to a working copy in the browser to keep memory usage reasonable, while the exporter can still produce a high-resolution PNG.

Frequently asked questions

About the Polaroid Generator

Is this Polaroid generator free?

Yes. It is free to use, adds no watermark and requires no account. You can create and download PNG images directly in the browser.

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. This version reads and processes the selected image with browser Canvas, so the image does not need to be uploaded to Siamese Cat Creative Club's server.

Which image formats are supported?

It accepts image formats supported by modern browsers, including JPG, PNG and WebP, and provides Square, Portrait and Wide Polaroid layouts.

Can I add a caption?

Yes. Add your own caption and choose a handwritten, serif, sans-serif or typewriter-style font. The tool uses fonts available on the visitor's device.

How can I create a vintage Polaroid look?

Try a little warmth, slightly lower saturation or contrast, a small amount of fade, film grain and the paper texture. A subtle frame rotation also helps create a casual scrapbook feel.

Why can very large phone photos be downscaled while editing?

Decoded high-resolution photos can consume a lot of browser memory. The tool limits the working copy to keep mobile performance stable, while still supporting high-resolution export.

Can I use the exported image commercially?

The tool itself does not add new restrictions to your image, but commercial use still depends on the rights to the source photo, logos, fonts or other content you use. Only use material you have permission to use.
