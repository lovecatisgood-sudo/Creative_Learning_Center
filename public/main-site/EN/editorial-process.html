Editorial process

How we create and check our family guides

Our goal is practical accuracy, not publishing volume. Each article should answer a real parent question and add something that comes from our location, service or first-hand operations.

Before publication

  • We define the reader, search intent and decision the page should support.
  • Operational statements are checked against current program pages, prices and service terms.
  • Health, development, nutrition and safety claims require an appropriate primary or authoritative source.
  • Thai and English versions are localized for natural reading rather than translated word for word.
  • A team member reviews facts, links, headings, images, metadata and the call to action before publishing.

Use of AI tools

AI tools may assist research organization, drafting, translation and editing. We do not publish unreviewed generated text. Service facts, prices and policies must come from verified project data, and unsupported information is removed or held for verification.

Corrections and updates

We update articles when packages, prices, policies or reliable external guidance changes. If you find an error, contact the team with the page URL and the detail that needs review.
