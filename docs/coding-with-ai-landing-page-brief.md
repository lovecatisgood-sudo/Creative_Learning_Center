- Primary query: AI coding classes for kids in Bangna
- Search intent: transactional course comparison and registration interest

- Immediate worry: AI may make coding seem either essential or pointless, while the parent cannot judge what a young child can realistically learn
- Answer needed: what the child will practise, what the 12 lessons cover, who teaches each language, the price, and how readiness is assessed
- Local relevance: Siamese Cat Creative Club serves families around Bang Kaeo, Bangna and Mega Bangna
- Original contribution: a concrete 12-lesson progression, two live studio games, named language-specific instructors, transparent prices, and a free readiness trial
- CTA pressure: transactional
- Thai counterpart: `/coding-with-ai`
- English counterpart: `/EN/coding-with-ai`

## Claim and source ledger

The page deliberately avoids claiming that coding guarantees intelligence, grades or brain development.

- Executive function includes working memory, mental flexibility and self-control: Center on the Developing Child at Harvard University, `https://developingchild.harvard.edu/resources/inbriefs/inbrief-executive-function/`
- A controlled study reported planning and inhibition gains after coding activities in first- and second-grade children: Arfe et al., 2019, `https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31920786/`
- A randomized trial compared unplugged programming, robot programming and usual kindergarten activities in children aged five to six: International Journal of STEM Education, 2024, `https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40594-024-00525-z`
- UNESCO frames student AI competence around understanding, applying and creating, with human agency, ethics, AI techniques and system design: `https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students`

The visible copy describes these findings as promising and explicitly states that individual cognitive or academic outcomes are not guaranteed.
