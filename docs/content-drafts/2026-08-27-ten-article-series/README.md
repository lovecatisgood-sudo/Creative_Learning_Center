## Publication gate

Before publishing any draft:

1. Remove the status, keyword and editorial-evidence sections from the visible article body.
2. Reconfirm prices, availability, ages, schedules and service policies with the operator.
3. Reconfirm every named competitor is operating and that its description remains current.
4. Add an original image or other approved first-hand anchor identified in the evidence notes.
5. Decide the truthful visible author or responsible organization.
6. Produce a naturally written Thai counterpart rather than a sentence-by-sentence translation.
7. Add reciprocal Thai/English hreflang through the existing bilingual blog record.
8. Preview the rendered HTML and verify that no Markdown syntax or editorial notes remain visible.
9. Measure progression to the linked program, contact or trial action.

## Language note

The Creative Club application currently supports only Thai and English. Its database has paired Thai/English content and publication fields, public pages generate Thai and `/EN` routes, and metadata exposes `th`, `en` and Thai `x-default` alternates. Adding the café site’s other five languages would require a separate application and CMS expansion before localized articles could be published correctly.
