Car Maze Privacy Notice

Car Maze is a programming-learning game for children. A parent must create and manage an account for players under 20.

What we use

For an account, we use the account owner's email, sign-in method, selected language, player nickname, game progress, policy consents and service-security data. We do not request a child's email, birth date, gender, precise location or advertising ID.

Guest mode uses a nickname and progress stored on the current device only. It does not require an email or sync across devices.

Optional play statistics

Only if you choose the optional analytics checkbox, the game may send anonymous event counts such as app opens, stage identifiers, hints and completion events, together with the platform. The game does not send the nickname or source code in these events. You can decline or change this choice on the device; declining does not block play.

Why we use it

Account data supports sign-in, progress sync, safety and account requests. Optional anonymous statistics help us understand aggregate game operation. Newsletters and partner offers are separate choices and are never required to play.

Your choices

An account owner can export data and delete the account from the game menu. Guest players can delete local progress from the game menu. Deletion is subject to information we must retain by law.

Service providers

Hosting, database, authentication and email providers may process data as necessary. The provider register and legal approvals must be completed before public release.
